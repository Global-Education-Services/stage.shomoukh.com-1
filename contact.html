CONVENIENT ACCESSIBILITY

Two Campuses in Prime Locations

Conveniently situated in Muscat's most family-friendly neighbourhoods with dedicated secure drop-off areas.

Al Mouj Muscat

Located right at the Al Mouj Main Community Hub, offering easy access for families residing in Al Mouj, Mawaleh, and Azaiba. Features extensive outdoor play gardens, specialized ateliers, and ample visitor parking.

Shatti Al Qurm

Situated in quiet Shatti Al Qurm on Al Suraj Street, adjacent to embassies and residential parks. Offers serene indoor gymnasiums, light ateliers, and shaded outdoor discovery courtyards.
