Online Enrollment Application

Complete the online enrollment form to apply for the academic year. Contact your preferred Al Mouj or Al Qurm campus to confirm eligibility and placement before completing your application.

OFFICIAL ENROLLMENT

Student Registration Form

Please fill out the details below carefully. Our admissions registrar will review your application and contact you within 24 hours.

1. Academic Year & Campus Selection

Published age bands are shown with each program. Admissions will confirm eligibility and placement for your intended start date.

2. Child Information
3. Parents / Guardian Information
4. Medical Form & Health History
5. Required Documents Checklist

Mark the documents you have ready. These boxes do not upload documents. Contact your selected campus to arrange document delivery; do not send passports or health records through the general inquiry form.

Need Help With Your Application?

Our admissions officers are available Sunday to Thursday, 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM to assist you.
